# Share to TV+ — Support

Share to TV+ sends a video from any app on your phone to your TV: share it to Share to TV+, tap your TV,
and it plays there. This page is where to get help with it.

## Getting started

- **Nothing to sign up for.** There's no account. Open the app and go.
- **Install Share to TV+ on your TV too**, if it's a Fire TV, Google TV or Android TV. It's the same
  app, and on a TV it becomes the player that waits for your phone. Open it once on the TV.
  Chromecasts and other smart TVs need nothing installed.
- **Put your phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi network.**
- **Share a video** from any app with its **Share** button and pick **Share to TV+**. No Share button?
  Copy the link and tap **+ ▸ Paste Link**.
- **Tap your TV.** The **Now Playing** bar at the bottom then lets you pause, seek, mute or stop.

## Common questions

### My TV doesn't show up

- Check the phone and TV are on the **same Wi-Fi network**. A guest network, or a mesh or router
  setting that keeps devices apart ("client isolation", "AP isolation"), hides TVs from each other.
- A **VPN on the phone** can stop it seeing anything on your home network. Turn it off and try again.
- On a Fire TV, Google TV or Android TV, **open Share to TV+ on the TV once**. After that, sending a
  video opens it for you.
- A TV you've sent to before is listed straight away, with a grey icon until it's found again.
  Tapping it still works if the TV is on.

### My TV is listed, but it's greyed out or says "YouTube videos only"

What a TV can play depends on what's on it:

| TV | What it can take |
|---|---|
| Fire TV, Google TV or Android TV **with Share to TV+ installed** | Everything |
| Chromecast, or a TV with Chromecast built in | Video links and YouTube |
| Other smart TVs with a YouTube app | YouTube videos only |

TVs that can't take anything are under **Other Devices**. Installing Share to TV+ on the TV is the way
to send it everything.

### It says "No videos found on this page"

Some pages build their video player in code, or only start loading the video once it's playing. Tap
**Not the right video? Look deeper** if it's there — the app loads the page in the background and
watches for the video. If it still finds nothing, **Report this link** and I'll take a look.

### It found a video, but not the right one

Pages often carry more than one video, including ads and previews. If the list has more than one,
pick the right one (tap a thumbnail to preview it on your phone), or **Look deeper** to find more.

### Instagram or Facebook videos don't work

Neither site shows its videos to anyone who isn't signed in. Sign in once in **Settings ▸ Signed-In
Sites**, or with the **Sign in to…** button the app shows for their links. You sign in on the site's
own page, and the app doesn't keep your password.

### YouTube opens in the TV's YouTube app, not Share to TV+

That's on purpose. It plays in the TV's own YouTube app

### A Netflix, Prime Video or Disney+ link only opens the app, not the show

Those links are handed to the TV's app for that service, since the video itself can only play there.
Some apps open straight to the show or movie; others only open to their home screen, depending on
the link. Let me know which service and link, and I'll see if it can go further.

### The video plays, but only while my phone is on

Some videos (ones from your phone's gallery, or from sites that only let the app that asked for them
play them) are passed along by your phone as they play. You'll see a notification while that
happens. Keep the phone on the same Wi-Fi until the video ends.

### There are no volume buttons in Now Playing

On a streaming stick (a Fire TV Stick, Chromecast with Google TV…) the TV's volume is set by the TV
itself, and the stick can't change it. Use the TV's remote for volume. **Mute** in Now Playing still
works.

### The video won't play on the TV

Some videos can't be sent at all: ones protected against copying (DRM), and ones whose link expires
quickly. If a video from a site that usually works won't play, **Report this link** from the app.
